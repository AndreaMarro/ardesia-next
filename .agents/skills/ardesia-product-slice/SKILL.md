# ardesia-product-slice

## Inputs
Read the complete canonical task and dependencies, relevant rows of docs/product-map.md, original D01–D68 criteria and scoped AGENTS. Identify user, need, observable behavior, source/revision, rights, constraints and one adverse case.

## Procedure
Deliver one complete vertical unit through the existing Core/Host/public contracts. A learning unit links objective, exact source, prediction, activity, explanation, evidence, feedback, recovery and transfer; quiz counts do not prove a course. Cover units, assumptions, limiting cases and counterexamples separately from UX. Use ardesia_science or ardesia_ux only for a concrete risk, with raw evidence and distinct context.

For REA first state the authorized product question. Observe a bounded donor, preserve provenance/version/rights, record REUSE/ADAPT/REPLACE/DEFER and test the real consumer. Prefer source/AST/tests; no proprietary copying or login/DRM/paywall bypass. Keep models separate from renderers and reuse the same persistence/SDK/Host.

## Output and errors
Return the usable capability, exact revision/evidence, migration/rollback and next eligible slice. Preserve DRAFT, privacy, consent/revocation and scientific revisions. Missing human review, device evidence or source rights remains pending. No invented mastery, efficacy, license or silent latest fallback. Post-M3/M4/M5 work follows actual dependencies across all 18 domains; dates do not accept milestones.

## Proof
Use risk-specific model calculations and real UI/consumer interactions, with adverse recovery and revocation paths. A new phase uses tools/status.mjs and its original gate; no second acceptance engine. D40 hardware, D65 Andrea and D66 five students require authentic observations. Technical completion, product acceptance and production authorization stay distinct.
